1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW

This technical documentation provides standardized chemical formulation models, rheological characteristics, and laboratory quality benchmarks for dry-mix mortars utilizing high-stability cellulose ethers (HPMC, HEMC) and redispersible polymer powders (RDP) produced by Dongyuan Shengda.

In cementitious composites such as tile adhesives, exterior wall putties, and self-leveling mortars, final mechanical properties depend directly on initial water retention kinetics. Cellulose ethers form an interlaced polymer lattice that traps free water via hydrogen bonding, preventing premature desiccation when applied over porous masonry substrates.

2. CORE CELLULOSE ETHER SPECIFICATION MATRIX

• SHENGDA SP100K (HPMC Flagship):
Viscosity: 90,000 to 110,000 mPa.s (Brookfield RVT 2% neutral solution, 20C).
Application: Standard and flexible tile adhesives (EN 12004 C1 and C2TE grades), delivering strong thixotropic anti-sag resistance and open time extension.

• SHENGDA SE100K (HEMC Flagship):
Viscosity: 90,000 to 110,000 mPa.s.
Application: High-temperature exterior wall renders and thin-set mortars. Features a thermal gelation point elevated up to 75C to 85C, resisting surface desiccation and skinning under intense summer sunlight.

• DY-WallPutty 40K:
Viscosity: 35,000 to 45,000 mPa.s.
Application: Interior and exterior skim coat putties, providing effortless trowel workability and a smooth, pinhole-free finish.

• DY-SelfLeveling 400:
Viscosity: 350 to 550 mPa.s.
Application: Self-leveling underlayments, preventing aggregate bleeding while ensuring uniform surface fluidity.

• Redispersible Polymer Powder (RDP / VAE):
Synthetic polymer resin providing flexural bond strength, substrate adhesion, and deformation buffer under thermal stress.
